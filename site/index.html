About this preview

Compatibility

Targets Windows 11 x64. iPhone connections may be intermittent; audio synchronization and device compatibility remain under testing.

Device checklist
Requirements and limits

No iPhone app required. Uses UxPlay to receive AirPlay on your local network. Protected video may not display. Mirroring only, not remote control.

Windows security warning

This preview is unsigned. Check the source and SHA-256 checksums before running it. Leave Windows security protections enabled.
